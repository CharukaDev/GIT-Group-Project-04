About PageTurner

Our Story

PageTurner was founded in 2018 by two lifelong book lovers who were frustrated by the lack of a trustworthy, community-driven bookshop. We started with a small warehouse, 500 books. Today we stock over 50,000 titles and serve readers across 30 countries.

We believe that every book deserves a passionate reader, and every reader deserves an honest recommendation. That is why we built a review platform alongside our shop — real reviews from real buyers, no sponsored placements, no hidden promotions.

Our Mission

  • Make quality books accessible and affordable to everyone.
  • Foster a community of honest, thoughtful readers.
  • Support independent authors and small publishers.
  • Deliver a seamless, trustworthy shopping experience.
